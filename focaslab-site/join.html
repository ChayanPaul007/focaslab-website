Join the lab

We work on control theory, formal methods, learning-based control and robotics. This page lists the topics we are currently recruiting for and explains how to apply.

Open topics

Current directions for new students and postdocs. Mention the topic when you write to us. Funding: PhD and M.Tech (Research) students receive the institute fellowship through IISc admissions; postdoctoral and project positions depend on available project funding.

How to apply

What to include when you write

  1. One or two sentences on your academic and professional background.
  2. Why you are writing, and which of our research or projects interests you.
  3. Your relevant skills and experience.
  4. A detailed CV that shows your academic record.

We read every message, but because of the number of applications we may not be able to reply to each one individually. If your interests match a current opening, we will contact you.
